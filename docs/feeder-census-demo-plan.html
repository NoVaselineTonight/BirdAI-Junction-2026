Your question: can smarter models train ours?

Yes, and the plan does exactly that. Three large models label your footage, and a model about 10 MB in size learns from those labels. It only has to know the handful of species at your feeders, which is why something that small can be accurate.

One correction. Memory is not what limits the board: it has about 1.3 GB free and the model needs a few tens of MB. Speed is the limit. The board has no AI chip, so one frame takes roughly 0.3 to 0.8 seconds. Counting needs a frame every couple of seconds, so that is enough.

Why keep a ready-made model too. Two small open models already exist that find birds and name all eight of the sketch's species without training. The demo is built on them first. Around 18:30 both are measured on unseen footage and the better one ships. If training goes wrong, you still have a demo.

Finnish birds and habitats

Your note changed four things, none of them the architecture.

  • Species list. 22 Finnish winter feeder species, each with its Finnish name: the sketch's eight (talitiainen, keltasirkku, sinitiainen, pikkuvarpunen, punatulkku, mustarastas, viherpeippo, varpunen) plus forest and town birds such as hömötiainen, töyhtötiainen, käpytikka, närhi, naakka and harakka.
  • Footage. Finnish or Nordic feeders, so the model learns the birds and backgrounds it will meet here.
  • Habitat mix. The page groups the half-month counts into forest, farmland, town and generalist birds. This is the sketch's own idea for saying something about the surroundings, and the page labels it as our grouping, not yet checked against Luomus.
  • No overclaiming. The page says one feeder is one data point. It reports counts and a species mix, not the state of an ecosystem.

Decisions I made for you

The sketch left these open. Say so if you want any of them different.

QuestionDecisionWhy
A video is minutes, the rule works in half-monthsSimulated clock: 1 second of footage = 1 minute, daylight 08–16 only. The page says so.Judges see hours and days fill up during a three-minute demo.
What is "surely identified"Confidence above a threshold that is measured tonight so that at least 90% of named birds are right. Others are drawn as "unidentified" and not counted.In monitoring data a wrong name is worse than no name.
One bad frameA count must hold in two consecutive frames."Largest number seen" is exactly the statistic a single false detection ruins.
How many photosOne per species per day, the frame with the most birds.The board has 3 GB free.
Zero or blankA watched hour with no birds is 0. An unwatched hour, or a species the model cannot name, is blank.The sketch's "a zero is a real zero".
How it runs on the boardPlain Python on the board's Linux side, viewed through the USB cable.The board's App Lab software is an old 2025 build; plain Python avoids that risk.
Where training runsVerda for everything tonight. Modal only if Verda has no machine.Setting up two clouds in one evening buys nothing.

Risks

The footage is the whole schedule. The model can only learn a species it has seen about 60 times or more. Late footage, or footage with two species in it, means a model that knows two species. If nothing arrives by about 17:00, the trained model will not make midnight and the demo ships with the ready-made models.

  • Accuracy is unknown until the first model is measured, around 16:00. Nobody has measured these models on Finnish feeder video. The plan measures it and shows the number. Tree sparrow against house sparrow will be the weak spot.
  • Labels come from models. The accuracy number is measured against teacher labels checked by a person, not against an expert's ground truth. The page and README say that.
  • Licence. The student is trained with Ultralytics, which is AGPL-3.0 and claims the trained weights. Fine for a hackathon and an open repo, a problem for a closed product. The ready-made models are Apache-2.0.
  • Small birds. If birds are tiny in the frame, neither model will name them. Footage where the feeder fills the picture matters more than resolution.
  • Still one feeder, recorded. The sketch's weak points on impact and on having no camera are not fixed by this plan. The honest sentence stays: "This is footage from a feeder, standing in for our camera."
